Kosmos

Kosmos is a private automation tool used by one person to run the YouTube channel UnCut Wisdom. It is not a public service, and nobody else can sign in to it.

What it does

  • Finds long-form videos that their owners have published under the Creative Commons Attribution licence.
  • Cuts short excerpts from them, adds captions and vertical framing, and credits the original creator in every description.
  • Uploads those excerpts to the operator's own YouTube channel through the YouTube Data API.
  • Reads view and subscriber counts for the operator's own channel to produce a daily report.
